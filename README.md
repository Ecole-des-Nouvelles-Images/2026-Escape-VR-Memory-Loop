## Introduction

**Memory Loop** is a single-player VR escape game in which you explore your environment, interact with the world around you, and solve puzzles to piece together the truth behind a tragedy buried in your past.

The game was created by third-year students at **L'École des Nouvelles Images** over a **five-week** production period. It was developed as the **final project of their academic year**, marking the validation of their bachelor's degree.

## Premise

You take on the role of a man on a mission: to unearth the deeply hidden memories he once put away.

The game is divided into **three parts**, each one delving deeper into the protagonist's psyche.

Will you accept the truth? Or will you be thrown into a **Memory Loop**?
